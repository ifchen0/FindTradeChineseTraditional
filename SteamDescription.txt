Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736861965]Find / Trade[/url] by Meme Goddess.

為 Meme Goddess 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736861965]Find / Trade[/url] 提供繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]Trade rules, modes and their tooltips
[*]Trade letters, mod settings and the Find / Trade tab
[*]Related defs (work type, job, trade filters)
[/list]
Terms follow the vanilla Traditional Chinese translation.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]交易規則、交易模式與說明
[*]交易信件、模組設定與「尋找／交易」分頁
[*]相關的 Def（工作類型、工作、交易篩選條件）
[/list]
用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after Find / Trade.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]A few labels (for example [i]Import: / Export: / Mode:[/i] and [i]Select traders[/i]) are hardcoded in the original mod and stay in English.
[*]The Find window and its filters come from TD Find Lib. Use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816260925]TD Find Lib - Chinese (Traditional)[/url] to translate that part.
[/list]
