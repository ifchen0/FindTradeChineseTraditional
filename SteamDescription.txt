Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736861965]Find / Trade[/url] by Meme Goddess, including the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3529443295]TD Find Lib - Continued[/url] library it is built on.

為 Meme Goddess 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736861965]Find / Trade[/url] 提供繁體中文翻譯，也包含它所使用的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3529443295]TD Find Lib - Continued[/url]。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]Find / Trade: trade rules, modes and their tooltips, trade letters, mod settings and the Find / Trade tab
[*]TD Find Lib: the Find window, the saved search library, all filter names, options and warnings
[*]Royalty, Ideology, Biotech and Odyssey filters (loaded only when that expansion is active)
[/list]
Terms follow the vanilla Traditional Chinese translation. Odyssey has no official Traditional Chinese translation, so its terms use common community names.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]Find / Trade：交易規則、交易模式與說明、交易信件、模組設定與「尋找／交易」分頁
[*]TD Find Lib：搜尋視窗、已儲存的搜尋庫、所有篩選條件的名稱、選項與警告
[*]皇權、理念、生機與漫遊的篩選條件（只在啟用該擴充時載入）
[/list]
用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯。漫遊沒有官方繁體中文翻譯，相關用詞採用社群常見譯名。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after Find / Trade.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]A few labels (for example [i]Import: / Export: / Mode:[/i] and [i]Select traders[/i]) are hardcoded in Find / Trade and stay in English.
[/list]
I made this because I wanted to play Find / Trade and no Traditional Chinese translation existed yet. If anyone would like to take over the translation, you are welcome to.
